### About me

I work as a Senior Developer and Product Owner at **Soramitsu Khmer Co., Ltd**, where I help design and ship backend systems for interbank payments, ledger services, and financial transaction processing.

My favorite kind of engineering is quiet, useful, and resilient: clean APIs, clear domain boundaries, reliable infrastructure, and systems that keep behaving well when the stakes are high.

### What I'm building

- **Financial infrastructure**: payment platforms, transaction processing, ledger services, and internal backend tooling.
- **Cambodia-focused products**: practical software for people visiting, living in, or building around Cambodia.
- **Open-source tools**: small, focused libraries and CLIs that solve real workflow problems.
- **Personal systems**: AI-assisted development, trading automation, self-hosted infrastructure, and better ways to learn in public.
